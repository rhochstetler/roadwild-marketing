About

Built by one person who got tired of six browser tabs.

Road Wild started because planning a week on the road meant an agency website for the campgrounds, a forum thread for the free spots, a maps app for the drive, and a screenshot folder for everything that wouldn't fit in any of them.

Where the map came from

Most of it began as public data — the National Park Service, the Recreation Information Database, OpenStreetMap and state land records — pulled in state by state and cleaned up. That's how you get to more than 20,000 spots without a decade of driving.

It's also why the honesty matters. A pin that came out of a database is a lead, not a promise, and Road Wild says which is which on every listing rather than letting you find out at the gate.

How it's being built

Three rules that haven't been broken yet

Nothing unbuilt gets promised

If a feature doesn't exist, it's marked in development wherever it's mentioned. That constraint costs conversions and it stays anyway.

No tracking

No analytics, no cookies, no advertising pixel on this site. The only measurement is a ?ref= tag on links posted by hand.

A failure never looks like a success

An empty list and a broken request are different things, and the app says which one you're looking at. It's the bug that has cost this project the most.

The beta is open for requests

No testimonials on this site yet, because there are no testers yet. You'd be among the first.
