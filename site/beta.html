What you're actually signing up for

There are no testimonials on this site because there are no testers yet — you'd be among the first. So here's the honest version instead.

  • A lot of the map has never been laid eyes on. Most pins came from public land records. They're real places, but nobody has confirmed you can sleep at all of them. Telling us which are wrong is the most useful thing a tester does.
  • Invites go out in batches, not instantly. The beta is small on purpose. This is a request, and it may be a while.
  • Some of it isn't built yet. Where that's true, the pricing page says so rather than implying otherwise.
  • We ask for an email address and nothing else. No tracking script, no cookie, no pixel. If you never get an invite you'll simply never hear from us again — and you can reply to any email to come off the list.
