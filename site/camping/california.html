Near Wilsonia, in the Grant Grove of Kings Canyon, you can pitch a tent among giant sequoias, trees that have been growing for well over a thousand years. In the morning the light comes down through them in long columns, and the General Grant Tree is a short walk away.

California has more places to sleep than anywhere else on Road Wild, and most are Forest Service and park campgrounds. On the east side of the Sierra, June Lake and Mammoth Lakes have campgrounds on cold, clear lakes off Highway 395, and Mono Hot Springs sits at the end of a long road past Big Creek. Bear Valley puts you on Lake Alpine, and the reservoirs above Pollock Pines are a summer favorite. In the south, Big Bear and Wrightwood are the mountain escapes, and Joshua Tree and Death Valley are winter country. High campgrounds open late, and fire season can close forests, so check before you go.
