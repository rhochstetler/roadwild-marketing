Camping in Colorado

572 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

Drive Trail Ridge Road between Estes Park and Grand Lake and you'll cross the Continental Divide on the highest paved through-road in the country, with tundra on both sides. Those two towns frame Rocky Mountain National Park, and in September the elk gather in Moraine Park and bugle at dusk, right by the campground.

Estes Park has the park's drive-in campgrounds. The Grand Lake side is mostly hike-in sites that need a permit. Further afield, Red Feather Lakes, Gunnison and Bayfield have Forest Service campgrounds, and out by Dinosaur the camps are on the Green and Yampa rivers, for float trips. Many high campgrounds don't open until June.
