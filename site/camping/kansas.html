Camping in Kansas

18 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

On the Cimarron National Grassland near Richfield, in the far southwest corner, you can still see the ruts left by wagons on the Santa Fe Trail, the longest stretch of the trail on public land. The grassland has a small campground, and the sky out there is enormous.

Most of the Kansas list is Corps of Engineers lakes. Perry Lake near Ozawkie and Perry, Tuttle Creek near Keats outside Manhattan, and Kanopolis Lake near Marquette all have campgrounds on the water. Near Kanopolis, Mushroom Rock State Park has sandstone formations that really do look like mushrooms.

What the badges mean

  • Verified by a nomad A Road Wild member reported on it from the ground.
  • Agency record From an official listing: Recreation.gov, the Forest Service or the National Park Service.
  • Overnight policy sourced We have a source for whether overnight stays are allowed.
  • Access checked Someone researched how you get in. Not a confirmation that overnight stays are allowed.
  • Listed business, not yet confirmed A private campground or RV park from map data. We have not yet confirmed it is open.
