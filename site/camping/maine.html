Camping in Maine

19 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

Lubec sits at the easternmost point of the United States, and from West Quoddy Head, with its red-and-white striped lighthouse, you can be among the first in the country to see the sun come up. Cobscook Bay State Park nearby has campsites on the water, where the tides rise and fall more than twenty feet.

The rest of Maine's short list is spread out. Machias has a few coastal sites, and Rangeley and Naples sit in lake country inland. Old Orchard Beach has a big campground near the sand south of Portland. Maine's camping season is short, roughly Memorial Day to mid-October.

What the badges mean

  • Verified by a nomad A Road Wild member reported on it from the ground.
  • Agency record From an official listing: Recreation.gov, the Forest Service or the National Park Service.
  • Overnight policy sourced We have a source for whether overnight stays are allowed.
