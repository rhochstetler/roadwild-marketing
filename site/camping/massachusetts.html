Camping in Massachusetts

36 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

Out at the end of Cape Cod, near Provincetown, the dunes run for miles and the light makes the whole place look painted. North Truro has campgrounds near the bay, and Wellfleet Hollow State Campground near North Eastham sits a short ride from the Cape Cod National Seashore and its ocean beaches.

Campgrounds on the Cape fill up fast in summer, so book ahead. Inland, Sturbridge has RV parks near Old Sturbridge Village, and Chester is a quiet base in the Berkshire hills. Plymouth and the towns south of Boston have a few lake campgrounds near the coast.

What the badges mean

  • Verified by a nomad A Road Wild member reported on it from the ground.
  • Agency record From an official listing: Recreation.gov, the Forest Service or the National Park Service.
  • Overnight policy sourced We have a source for whether overnight stays are allowed.
  • Access checked Someone researched how you get in. Not a confirmation that overnight stays are allowed.
  • Listed business, not yet confirmed A private campground or RV park from map data. We have not yet confirmed it is open.
