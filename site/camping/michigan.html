Camping in Michigan

573 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

Most of Michigan's listings are in the Upper Peninsula. Munising, at Pictured Rocks, has the biggest group, from Pictured Rocks backcountry sites and Hiawatha National Forest campgrounds to private parks in town. Grand Marais, Newberry and Manistique have a run of small state forest campgrounds on lakes and rivers, often with just a handful of sites each.

In the Lower Peninsula, Baldwin and Mio sit in the Huron-Manistee National Forest, and private RV parks make up most of the rest. St. Ignace is the easy stop at the bridge. Expect bugs in June and full campgrounds on summer weekends.
