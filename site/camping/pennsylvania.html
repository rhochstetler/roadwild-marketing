Camping in Pennsylvania

353 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

Pennsylvania's listings are mostly private, paid campgrounds spread across the state. The biggest cluster is around Fayetteville and Mont Alto in Michaux State Forest, where there are numbered primitive sites alongside the private campgrounds near Gettysburg.

Up in the northwest, Marienville sits in the Allegheny National Forest, with Forest Service recreation areas and an equestrian campground among the listings. Lancaster County, around Conestoga, has private campgrounds within reach of the farm country. Only a handful of listings are agency records, so it's worth checking hours and seasons before you go.
