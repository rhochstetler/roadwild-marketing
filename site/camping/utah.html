Camping in Utah

490 places to stay, each with its nearest town and where our information comes from.

Highway 128 out of Moab follows the Colorado River between red cliffs, and the small campgrounds along it put you right at the water with the Fisher Towers up the road. It's one of the prettiest drives in the West, and you can stop for the night.

If Moab feels busy, head for the San Rafael Swell around Temple Mountain, or up into the Henry Mountains outside Hanksville, said to be the last range mapped in the lower 48. Springdale is the gateway to Zion, and Flaming Gorge in the northeast has cool campgrounds above a red-rock reservoir. Desert summers are hot, so go high in July and August.
