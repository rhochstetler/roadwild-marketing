Features

What's actually in it today

Everything below works right now. Anything still being built is on the pricing page, marked as such.

The sleep spot map

More than 20,000 overnight spots across all 50 states: dispersed camping, free camps, established campgrounds, RV parks, rest areas and retail overnight.

  • Filter by what you sleep in — a 34ft rig and a hatchback don't want the same pins
  • Road access and surface, so you know what you're driving in on
  • A provenance badge on every listing: imported, or confirmed by a nomad

Things to do nearby

Hikes, hot springs, swimming holes, laundromats, the good coffee — attached to where you're already going to be, not to a city you'd have to drive into.

  • Rated by the people who actually went
  • Search by what you feel like doing, not just by place name

Trip planning

Build a route stop by stop, or give the planner your dates, your pace and what you like doing and let it draft the week for you.

  • Save routes and come back to them
  • Check in at stops as you go

The community

Boards by topic, direct messages, and opt-in location sharing so you can find nomads nearby — only when you want to be found.

  • Block and report anyone, and moderators actually read it
  • No feed, no follower counts, no algorithm
