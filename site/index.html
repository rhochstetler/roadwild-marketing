What it is

Three things, and it does all three offline of each other.

The map

Sleep spots with the part that actually matters: whether you can stay the night, what the road in is like, and how sure we are. Filter by what you sleep in — a 34ft rig and a hatchback do not want the same pins.

Things to do

Hikes, hot springs, swimming holes, laundromats, the good coffee — attached to the places you're already going to be, not to a city you'd have to drive into.

People

A board and direct messages for people who are actually out here. Who's nearby, what a road is like this week, which spot got posted and ruined. No feed, no follower counts.

Before you put your name in

What you're actually signing up for.

No testimonials on this page, because there are no testers yet — you would be among the first. So here is the honest version instead.

  • A lot of the map has never been laid eyes on. Most pins came from public land records and agency data. They are real places, but nobody has confirmed you can sleep at all of them. Telling us which ones are wrong is the single most useful thing a tester does.
  • Invites go out in batches, not instantly. The beta is small on purpose. Putting your name in is a request, and it may be a while.
  • Some of it is not built yet. Where that's true, the pricing below says so rather than implying otherwise. Nothing on this page is a feature that does not exist.
  • We ask for an email address and nothing else. No tracking script, no cookie, no advertising pixel anywhere on this site. If you never get an invite, you'll simply never hear from us again — and you can reply to any email to be taken off the list.
